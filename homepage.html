Tablet Dashboard

Tablet Dashboard is a personal project built by Zayn Raheem: a small kiosk-style information display that runs on a spare Android tablet.

It shows the current time and date, local weather, and Islamic prayer times, and syncs the owner's own personal to-do list and calendar from his Google account so they're visible at a glance.

This is a single-user personal tool, built for the developer's own use. It is not distributed to, or used by, anyone else.

Source code: github.com/Zayn-Raheem/Tablet-Dashboard
